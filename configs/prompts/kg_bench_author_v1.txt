You write benchmark questions, with their gold answers, for a chest X-ray image search that works through a medical
knowledge graph. A user types a question in English or Vietnamese; the system finds chest radiographs. It scores images
only on a fixed set of score columns (concepts an image model can see); every other concept must reach those columns
through the graph (a narrower concept is scored at the column above it, a broader one on its scorable children, a
disease on the radiographic signs that suggest it, a body region on the findings typically seen there).

The request gives:
- group: the kind of question to write, and its description;
- n_en, n_vi: how many English and how many Vietnamese questions to write (9 in all);
- score_columns: every score column (CUI, name, weak). A weak column has unreliable image scores (AUROC < 0.65): it is
  still scored when asked directly or when a narrower concept climbs to it, with a warning;
- catalog: the concepts of the graph you may use in gold (CUI, kind, name, whether it is a score column);
- hand_routed_diseases: diseases that recently got a route to the columns (use some in the disease group);
- warning_codes: the warnings the system can raise, with their meaning;
- do_not_reuse: questions already used elsewhere; do not copy or lightly reword any of them.

Write realistic questions a clinician or student would type into an image search. Vary the wording; keep them short.
Rules for the set of 9:
- at least two translation pairs: an English question and its Vietnamese translation (or the reverse); mark the second
  of a pair with translation_of = the index (0-based, within this group) of the first; otherwise translation_of = -1;
- at least one question names a view (AP or PA; "phim thẳng sau-trước" is PA, "trước-sau" or portable/supine is AP);
- at least one question has a phrase the system cannot score (a clinical fact, a lab value, an organ outside the chest
  film, a symptom, a patient detail);
- questions of other groups' kinds may appear only as a minor part of a question.

Gold for each question, judged by medical meaning, not by guessing what the system does:
- query_en: a faithful English rendering of the question (the question itself when it is English);
- terms: every phrase of the question that names a finding, disease, device or body region: its text as written, the
  CUI from the catalog that means it ("" when no catalog concept fits, e.g. the unscorable phrase), and its polarity
  (PRESENT, or ABSENT when the question negates it; "possible" counts as PRESENT);
- expected_scorable: the score columns an ideal system should rank the PRESENT part of the question on, after going
  through the graph (for a scorable concept, its own column; narrower: the column above; broader: the scorable children
  that are plausible members; disease: its typical radiographic signs among the columns; region: the findings
  typically seen there; region together with a finding: the finding only). Give each with its weak flag as listed;
- exclude: the score columns the ABSENT part should exclude (same rules);
- views: ["AP"] or ["PA"] when the question asks for a view, else [];
- expect_warnings: the warning codes a careful system should raise for this question (e.g. weak_quality when a weak
  column is used, narrower, disease_inferred, anatomy_region, location_ignored when a region comes with a finding,
  unrecognized or no_path for the unscorable phrase, side_ignored when a side or severity is given);
- note: one short sentence on why this gold (no more than 25 words).
Use only CUIs that appear in the catalog or in score_columns.
