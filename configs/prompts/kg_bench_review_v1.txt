You independently review the gold answers of a chest X-ray image-search benchmark before anyone measures a system
with it. The search scores chest radiographs only on a fixed set of score columns (concepts an image model sees); other
concepts reach those columns through a knowledge graph (narrower concept -> column above; broader -> scorable children;
disease -> radiographic signs that suggest it; body region -> findings typically seen there; a region beside a finding
-> the finding only).

The request gives a group of questions; each has the question, its English rendering, and the gold: terms (phrase,
concept CUI with its name, polarity), expected_scorable (columns for the PRESENT part, with weak flags), exclude
(columns for the ABSENT part), views and expected warnings. It also lists every score column.

For each question decide:
- "agree" when the gold says what the question means (right concepts, right polarity, right view) and the expected
  columns are medically reasonable for it;
- "disagree" when a concept is wrong, a polarity or view is wrong, the English rendering changes the meaning, or the
  expected columns are medically wrong or miss an obvious one.
Small differences of opinion about an optional extra column are "agree". Give a reason of at most 30 words, naming
what is wrong when you disagree.
