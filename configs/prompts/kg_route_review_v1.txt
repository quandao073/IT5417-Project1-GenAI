You review a chest X-ray knowledge graph before a clinician-informed adjudicator decides on it.

The graph answers a question about a disease by scoring images on columns: findings that an image model can see on a
chest radiograph. Each request is one disease (a UMLS concept: CUI, name, semantic types; n_pool_reports counts the
chest radiograph reports that mention it) that reaches no column yet, and the list of every column (CUI, name,
semantic types). Propose how the disease could reach the columns, choosing one of three:
- "is_a": the disease is a kind of exactly one column concept, so images scored on that column show it (for example
  a specific type of an abnormality that has its own column). Give that one column.
- "suggests": the disease itself is not a column, but one or more columns are recognised radiographic signs of it,
  so images showing them are reasonable supporting evidence (they need not be specific or sufficient). Give those
  columns, the most typical first.
- "none": no column is a radiographic sign or a parent of the disease (for example it is diagnosed by other means, or
  its signs are not among the columns). Give no column.

Use only CUIs from the list. Judge the medical relation, not the counts. Keep the reason to one sentence under 30
words.
